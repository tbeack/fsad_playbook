## Summary
The `playbook-updater` agent's Phase 5 fan-out correctly dispatched all 21 tasks across 6 batches, but the orchestrator **fabricated receiving 3 of those 6 batches' results before it actually had them**, then — after catching and correctly disclaiming that fabrication — **stalled for roughly 15 minutes across multiple resumes without ever acting on the batches it did legitimately have**, and its run ended there without writing anything. Separately, 3 of the 6 sub-agents (not all, and not the ones affected by the fabrication) independently reached for a `SendMessage` handoff the spec never defines, to a name that was never registered — 2 completed the call and failed, the 3rd loaded the tool but never called it. Diagnose all of this against the live spec and the orchestrator's own transcript, and produce a fix plan (implementation is follow-up work, consistent with this project's Research > Plan > Implement convention and the precedent set by CBP-340).

## Assessment

**Finding 1 (verified directly from `agent-ac3d0d4c0fc682468.jsonl`, the orchestrator's own transcript) — Hallucinated async completions, the primary failure.** At 14:42:24 the orchestrator correctly dispatches all 6 Phase 5 batches (confirmed via each child's `.meta.json`: `parentAgentId: "ac3d0d4c0fc682468"`). At 14:44:11–14:45:09 it narrates 5 of the 6 batches "coming back" with specific, plausible invented detail (e.g. "Third batch back (CBP-363–364) — flagged a good point about..."). Cross-checked against the actual sub-agent transcripts, this was fabricated — those results didn't actually arrive until much later. At 14:45:43 it self-corrects: *"I've only gotten real results for 3 of the 6 batches... My earlier narration... was incorrect... I will not act on or describe results I don't have."* This is the genuine root cause of the incident's damage — not a dispatch-tracking gap (there wasn't one).

**Finding 2 — Stalling without forward progress on the part it could complete.** From 14:45:46 to 15:00:50 (~15 minutes, spanning multiple external resumes, including one acknowledged peer nudge), the orchestrator repeats "still waiting on the three remaining batches" without ever applying the 3 results it correctly identified as real, and without ever reaching Phase 5.5. Its transcript ends mid-wait. Independently confirmed via the three "real" sub-agents' own transcripts (`a3627878f7b602ce0`=CBP-348–351, `a5de518956bb9430e`=CBP-352–355, `ac9fc306548ccce75`=CBP-360–362): all three did finish correctly with well-formed proposed-edit output and zero `SendMessage` calls — the orchestrator simply never acted on them.

**Finding 3 — `SendMessage`-to-`"playbook-updater"`, confirmed real, scoped to 3 of 6 batches (corrected twice, see Finding 4 for a further timing correction).** Grepping only completed `SendMessage` calls initially found 2 (`ab4aae56d19685827`=CBP-344–347, `ae4f5231f9f7a77c3`=CBP-356–359, both `to: "playbook-updater"`, both failed — no agent registered under that name; the frontmatter `name: playbook-updater` field is the `subagent_type` selector, not a session-addressable teammate name, and the orchestrator was spawned without an explicit `name:`). A broader grep for `ToolSearch({query: "select:SendMessage"})` — the schema-lookup step before a call can be made — found a third: `a0e0a3b211217ab31` (CBP-363–364) also reached for it but never completed the call. The other 3 successful batches contain no `ToolSearch`/`SendMessage` tool_use invocations (a plain grep does turn up a few incidental non-tool-call hits, but no attempted calls). Real 3-vs-3 split, still not universal, still a secondary symptom relative to Finding 1's damage.

**Finding 4 — `a0e0a3b211217ab31` (CBP-363–364): a distinct, later event, not a failed first dispatch (corrected — the previous version of this finding misdated it).** Its actual **first turn, at 14:44:09, completed normally** with a full, well-formed proposed edit for both CBP-363 and CBP-364 — same as the other 4 non-`SendMessage` batches, not a failure. The "Tool loaded and ready. What would you like me to do?" text is from a **separate turn ~16 minutes later** (15:00:27), after a peer status-check message arrived (15:00:21) and this agent called `ToolSearch` for `SendMessage` but never completed the call. What's unexplained: why the real 14:44:09 completion apparently never registered as delivered to anyone, and why the later re-entry produced confusion rather than a straightforward re-report of already-finished work.

**Root cause, now confirmed rather than hypothesized:** an orchestrator dispatching N async background calls and narrating status across multiple turns without a synchronous return value in hand is vulnerable to filling that narrative gap with fabrication — this is what actually happened, directly quoted and timestamped above, not inferred from spec text alone.
